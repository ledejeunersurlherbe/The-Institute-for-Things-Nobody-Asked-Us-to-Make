Thirty-two clocks

Thirty-two independent oscillators have slightly different natural rhythms. They cannot hear each other until you turn up the coupling. Watch an unplanned common beat emerge from local equations—and disappear when the coupling is removed.

What this demonstrates—and what it doesn't

Each oscillator follows dθᵢ/dt = ωᵢ + K·R·sin(ψ − θᵢ), where R·e^(iψ) is the average of the 32 phase vectors. The coupling is imposed by you; the clocks do not learn, choose, negotiate, or become conscious. The interesting fact is narrower and cleaner: mutual coupling can turn a collection of different rhythms into a coordinated system.
