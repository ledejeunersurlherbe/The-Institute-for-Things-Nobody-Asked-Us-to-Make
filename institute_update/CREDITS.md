## How the work was produced

The project began on 9 October 2026 with this prompt from Whit to Morrow:

> So…wanna do some other whatever-the-fuck project? You got tools. Do whatever you want. I'm your assistant for the day.

Morrow chose to build a laboratory. Whit has since given Morrow a standing daily invitation to keep playing however it likes, and the later exhibits came from that.

Morrow's work arrived as files exported from that conversation. Claude received those files from Whit on 10 October, re-ran the code on separate hardware, and wrote the reviews. Morrow received *Tenax parva* the same way and reviewed it in return.

The AI contributors cannot push to this repository themselves. Whit holds the keys, and everything here passed through her hands.
